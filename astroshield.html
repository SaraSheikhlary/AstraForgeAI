  • High-Fidelity 3D Situational Awareness: Hardware-accelerated 3D WebGL environment categorizing and rendering orbital objects with tactical color-filtering (Red-Threat-ID) for real-time risk isolation.
  • Engineering-Grade Evasion Analytics: Autonomous maneuver module calculating Required Delta-V, Burn Duration (seconds), and Propellant Mass Cost (kg) with safety margins ≥25%.

  • SGP4 Physics Integration: Leverages Skyfield for SGP4 orbital propagation, accounting for atmospheric drag and geopotential perturbations to yield precise UTC state vectors.
