AstroShield AI

AstroShield AI is a production-grade, decoupled digital twin platform engineered for real-time orbital flight dynamics, Space Traffic Management (STM), and conjunction risk assessment. Built to bridge theoretical orbital mechanics with cloud-native microservice architecture, the platform models high-density satellite constellations and dynamic space debris fields to evaluate collision risks and compute optimal evasion maneuvers under live hardware constraints.

Key Technical and Architectural Features

1. Physically Grounded Telemetry and Risk Analytics Engine

  • Universal CCSDS and CelesTrak Ephemeris Data Ingestion: Parses and indexes real-time Two-Line Element (TLE) catalogs and standardized Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems (CCSDS) Orbit Ephemeris Messages (OEMs) tracking >15,000 active payloads, rocket bodies, and debris objects (including commercial mega-constellations such as Starlink).
  • Asynchronous Microservice Task Execution: Powered by FastAPI, Celery distributed task workers, and Redis for non-blocking, multi-threaded ephemeris ingestion and background orbit propagation.
  • Sub-Millisecond Spatial Screening: Leverages optimized SciPy cKDTree spatial partitioning structures to execute sub-millisecond 3D spatial neighbor lookups across multi-shell orbital populations, eliminating O(N^2) computational bottlenecks.
  • Two-Stage TCA and Conjunction Assessment: Implements a two-stage temporal propagation pipeline (coarse screening followed by local numerical refinement) achieving strict IEEE 754 float64 machine precision limits (machine epsilon approximately 2.22 x 10^-16) and exact miss-distance convergence (450 meters). Aligns with NASA/CARA risk engine standards to evaluate actionable collision probabilities (Pc >= 10^-4).
  • Closed-Loop Maneuver Optimization: Computes optimal impulsive evasion burns (Delta-v) and propellant safety budgets via a Monte Carlo stochastic simulation engine (N = 2,000 ensemble scale) and constrained SLSQP optimization. Validated on historical benchmark case CDM-2026-0892, automatically reducing an actionable Pc = 5.56 x 10^-3 down to a negligible 6.23 x 10^-24 via an optimal 0.145 m/s Delta-v burn vector (Delta-v = [0.104, 0.090, 0.048]T m/s).

2. Decoupled Enterprise Architecture, Security, and Chaos Resilience

  • Containerized Microservice & Self-Contained Frontend Deployment: Features a self-contained, high-performance Streamlit frontend dashboard paired with an asynchronous containerized FastAPI backend, ensuring instantaneous UI responsiveness, robust 3D WebGL rendering, and dedicated individual asset telemetry inspection capabilities.
  • High-Concurrency and Chaos Resilience: Subjected to rigorous Pytest chaos injection and high-frequency telemetry loads, achieving a 100% success rate across system health checks, malformed telemetry resilience, corrupt coordinate fallback, and immutable audit logging (with response latencies <120 ms).
  • IP and Codebase Protection: Employs a zero-trust, decoupled deployment strategy where proprietary numerical solver pipelines, spatial indexing modules, and raw telemetry definitions remain entirely isolated within secure, independent repositories.

3. Production Health and Persistent State Management

  • Automated Keep-Alive Monitoring: Integrated with scheduled HTTP cron execution to maintain persistent container warmup and guarantee 24/7 service availability.

4. Operational Latency and Ephemeris Synchronization Profile

  • Initial Connection Latency: Near-instantaneous page loading and session initialization, enabled by the self-contained frontend architecture running directly on Streamlit Cloud without external backend spin-down delays.
  • Telemetry Refresh Bounds: Telemetry synchronization frequency is dictated by upstream CelesTrak ephemeris updates and background cKDTree index reconstruction, prioritizing backend mathematical rigor and numerical precision over instantaneous client-side rendering.
